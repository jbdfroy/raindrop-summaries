    4. Empowering the president to impose climate-minded tariffs on imported goods as a first step toward creating a carbon adjustment mechanism at the U.S border

    6. Narrowing the scope of existing tariff authorities, such as Section 232, to prevent overreach and ensure respect for trading partners’ interests
    7. Pursuing domestic reforms alongside multilateral trade system reform or integrating new tariff regulations into foreign trade agreements with like-minded economies
