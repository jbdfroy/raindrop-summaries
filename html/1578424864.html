    Titre : The Geopolitical Impact of Russia’s Invasion of Ukraine on Its Global Position

    Source : Foreign Affairs (December 2025)

    Article : In 2022, Russia was in a decent global position with strong partnerships, extensive economic ties, and a working relationship with key powers. However, its invasion of Ukraine led to adversarial relationships with Europe and the United States, increased reliance on China, and reduced influence beyond its neighborhood. The war has consumed Russia’s attention and military capacity, hindering its ability to influence events elsewhere.

    Russia’s overestimation of its hard power was first evident in 2014 when it invaded Crimea and the Donbas, undermining pro-Russian sentiment in Ukraine. Since then, Moscow has adapted its foreign policy by evading export controls, increasing oil sales to India, and forging deeper relations with China. It also began favoring Iran over Israel in the Middle East.

    Despite these shifts, Russia has lost much of its capacity to protect its partners and interests beyond Ukraine. It was a bystander as Azerbaijan seized Nagorno-Karabakh from Armenia, Russia’s traditional ally, and as local rebels overthrew the Assad regime in Syria.

    In 2024, Trump’s reelection was celebrated in Moscow, but his disdain for international law and affinity for spheres of influence has not advantageously positioned Russia. The United States joined Israel in damaging Iran’s military and nuclear infrastructure, extracted Maduro from power, and increased its support for Ukraine.

    Russia remains influential in the Middle East and Africa but has yet to gain advantages from the tensions between Washington and European capitals. Europe is increasing its own support for Ukraine, and NATO remains a functioning institution with which Russia must reckon. Putin’s obsession with Ukraine persists, and he may consider escalating the conflict beyond Ukraine’s borders.

    Europe and the United States should step up their support for Ukraine while preparing for Russian escalation in and around Ukraine. They should also refrain from rushing talks to end the conflict, mindful of their collective power in an anarchic world order.
