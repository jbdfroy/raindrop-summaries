    The article discusses the importance of maintaining the independence of federal agencies such as the Federal Reserve and the Bureau of Labor Statistics for the health of the U.S. economy. The Fed’s independence allows it to make decisions about interest rates and the money supply free from political pressure, which is crucial for businesses to plan with confidence. The article also mentions the importance of reliable, apolitical government data and the negative consequences of disinformation for businesses.

    The article highlights the challenges faced by business leaders in speaking out against the administration’s actions due to fear of repercussions, such as losing federal funding or contracts. However, it argues that business leaders across industries share a common interest in preserving a well-functioning operating environment and should coordinate their efforts to defend against the erosion of foundational issues.

    The article suggests that trusted organizations with business leaders in their ranks, such as the Business Roundtable, the Chief Executive Leadership Institute, or the Leadership Now Project, should discreetly convene influential executives to develop a coordinated defense strategy. But action cannot be confined to meetings behind closed doors; business leaders must also use their voice in the public sphere.
